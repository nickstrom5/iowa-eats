Checking an Iowa restaurant inspection

The Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL) inspects restaurants in most counties, and ten local agencies inspect the rest. All of them post their reports on DIAL's statewide inspection search. Iowa posts no score, grade or placard.

DIAL's inspection search

What the reports show

Search a restaurant by name to see its inspections: the date and type of each visit, the jurisdiction, and the violations, split into foodborne-illness risk factors and good retail practices. The reports are a snapshot of the day of the inspection. Iowa Plates computes no grade of its own and doesn't rank by inspections.

What Iowa Plates does show

Every place in the app and on the web search links to DIAL's inspection search. The rest is hand-checked research and James Beard honors.
