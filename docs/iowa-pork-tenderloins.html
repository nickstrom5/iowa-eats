Pork tenderloins

9 places for a breaded pork tenderloin, each a winner or finalist in the Iowa Pork Producers Association's judged contest in 2025 or 2026.

A contest since 2003

The Iowa Pork Producers Association has held its breaded pork tenderloin contest every year since 2003. Its 2026 champion is Wasted Grain in Carroll, with Friend's Hide-A-Way in Underwood as runner-up; Hometown Heroes in Grinnell won in 2025. Indiana claims the sandwich too, so we don't say who invented it.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Redfield (1), Underwood (1), Grinnell (1), Williamsburg (1), Waverly (1), Council Bluffs (1), North Liberty (1), Salix (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (the Iowa Pork Producers Association's contest results, a dated story, or Travel Iowa's own page or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, Iowa Plates orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
